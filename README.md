## Open-Source GitHub Projects



### Marketing Automation Platforms



- **[Mautic](https://github.com/mautic/mautic)**

  **The leading open-source marketing automation platform**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **6,000+ GitHub stars** and **200,000+ organizations** using it . **Email campaigns, landing pages, forms, lead scoring, segmentation, and CRM** . **Multi-channel marketing automation** — email, social, and SMS via plugins . **Self-hosted or cloud** . **The de facto open-source HubSpot and Klaviyo alternative** . **Best for comprehensive marketing automation**.



- **[Listmonk](https://github.com/knadh/listmonk)**

  **The leading open-source newsletter and mailing list manager**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **15,000+ GitHub stars** . **Self-hosted with a single binary** — no dependencies except PostgreSQL . **High-performance** — millions of subscribers on modest hardware . **Campaign management, subscriber segmentation, templates, bounce processing, and analytics** . **The de facto open-source Mailchimp alternative** . **Best for newsletters and mailing lists**.



- **[Keila](https://github.com/keila-io/keila)**

  **Open-source newsletter tool with visual editor**, AGPL-3.0 licensed with **2,000+ GitHub stars** . **Modern UI with drag-and-drop email builder** . **Self-hosted via Docker** . **Best for modern newsletter management**.



- **[Mailtrain](https://github.com/Mailtrain-org/mailtrain)**

  **Self-hosted newsletter application**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **Manage large subscriber lists with segmentation** . **Best for high-volume self-hosted newsletters**.



### Customer Engagement & Support



- **[Chatwoot](https://github.com/chatwoot/chatwoot)**

  **Open-source customer engagement suite**, MIT licensed with **20,000+ GitHub stars** . **Live chat, email, SMS, WhatsApp, and social media in one inbox** . **Shared inboxes, canned responses, and automation** . **Self-hosted or cloud** . **The de facto open-source Intercom and Zendesk alternative** . **Best for customer support and engagement**.



- **[Novu](https://github.com/novuhq/novu)**

  **Open-source notification infrastructure**, MIT licensed with **35,000+ GitHub stars** . **Unified API for email, SMS, push, in-app inbox, Slack, Teams, Discord, and WhatsApp** . **Visual workflow editor** with conditions, delays, and digest . **Subscriber preference management** . **Best for multi-channel notifications**.



- **[Apprise](https://github.com/caronc/apprise)**

  **Push notification library for 100+ services**, MIT licensed with **12,000+ GitHub stars** . **One API for Discord, Slack, Telegram, email, SMS, and more** . **CLI and Python library** . **Best for multi-service notifications**.



### Event Tracking & Analytics



- **[Jitsu](https://github.com/jitsucom/jitsu)**

  **Open-source data collection platform**, MIT licensed with **3,500+ GitHub stars** . **Collect event data and send to any destination** — databases, warehouses, and APIs . **Segment alternative** . **Best for event data pipelines**.



- **[PostHog](https://github.com/PostHog/posthog)**

  **Open-source product analytics**, MIT licensed with **20,000+ GitHub stars** . **Event tracking, funnels, session replay, feature flags, and A/B testing** . **Self-hosted or cloud** . **The de facto open-source Amplitude alternative** . **Best for product analytics**.



- **[Matomo](https://github.com/matomo-org/matomo)**

  **Open-source web analytics**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **20,000+ GitHub stars** . **Full-featured with session recording and heatmaps** . **Best for comprehensive analytics**.



- **[Plausible](https://github.com/plausible/analytics)**

  **Lightweight and privacy-friendly analytics**, AGPL-3.0 licensed . **No cookies, no tracking** . **Best for simple analytics**.



- **[Umami](https://github.com/umami-software/umami)**

  **Privacy-friendly analytics**, MIT licensed with **25,000+ GitHub stars** . **Simple, fast, and privacy-focused** . **Best for privacy-first analytics**.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Customer engagement platforms handle personal data and communication preferences. Self-hosted solutions require proper security hardening, access controls, and compliance with data privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, CAN-SPAM, ePrivacy).

- **Email deliverability requires IP reputation management** — self-hosted email platforms must warm up IPs, configure SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and monitor blacklists . Commercial platforms provide managed deliverability.

- **Push notification delivery depends on platform providers** — APNs (Apple) and FCM (Google) mediate mobile push. Self-hosted platforms still require these services .

- **License considerations**: Mautic uses GPL-3.0, Listmonk uses AGPL-3.0, Chatwoot uses MIT, PostHog uses MIT, and Novu uses MIT. Verify licensing against your use case before committing.

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong marketing automation, notification, and analytics foundations, but **AI-powered optimization, real-time orchestration, and vendor-supported SLAs** remain primarily commercial offerings.
